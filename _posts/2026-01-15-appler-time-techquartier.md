## ÄpplerTime @ TechQuartier in Frankfurt 🥨

<!-- excerpt-start -->

Following the launch of the European "Sovereign" Cloud by Amazon Web Services (AWS), the timing was perfect for a discussion on data & AI sovereignty in the EU 🤝

<!-- excerpt-end -->

House was full, feedback was great, and the Äppler was better than my colleague Florian let on 🍏

A huge thanks to my fellow panelists Michael Marquardt (hessian.AI), Red Boumghar (Celestical - European Infrastructure for AI Agents), Thimo Groneberg (Polarise), and Tobias Weitzel (confora labs) for promoting a deep, lively, and light-hearted discussion 🙏

Navigating data sovereignty is a game, not a nuisance — if you don't play it, everyone loses; if you get to the end, everyone wins 🌟
